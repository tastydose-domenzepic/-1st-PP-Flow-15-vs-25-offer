Method and caveats

  • Rates use delivered, not sent — open rate, click rate and placed order rate are all divided by delivered, matching how Klaviyo reports them. Recipients are shown separately.
  • Opens and clicks are unique — one profile counted once per email, not raw event totals.
  • Sessions started is a proxy. Klaviyo has no native session metric on flows. This column is unique profiles who fired Active on Site inside the attribution window after the send. Treat it as directional, and expect it to run close to unique clicks.
  • Placed orders are attributed conversions on the Shopify Placed Order metric. Order rate is orders ÷ delivered; the buyer count (unique converters) is lower where one profile ordered twice.
  • The 15% arm carries extra SHORT-format sends in DACH and HU that the 25% arm does not have. The headline verdict and the arm chart use Standard-format sends only so the arms are comparable; the Format filter lets you see it either way.
  • Revenue is post-discount order value as recorded by Shopify. It is not margin — the extra 10 points of discount on the 25% arm still has to be paid for out of gross margin, so run this through the contribution margin model before treating 25% as the winner commercially.
  • Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates open rates unevenly across markets. Click and order rates are the reliable comparison.
