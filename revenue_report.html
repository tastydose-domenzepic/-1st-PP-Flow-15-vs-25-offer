Value sitting in carts and checkouts

Currency warning — do not blend these

Klaviyo returns Placed Order revenue in EUR for every market, but the Added to Cart and Started Checkout values come through in local presentment currency: forints for HU and koruna for CZ. The per-market figures below are shown in their native currency and must not be summed. Divided out per cart they all land around €100–111, which is what confirms the currency reading. Only the EUR revenue column is safe to aggregate.

Cross-check

Converge, at flow level

Converge maps Klaviyo down to the flow, not the individual email, so this cannot be split per email — and it covers the whole flow (#1–#27), not just #16+. Its value is calibration.

The attribution gap on revenue

Klaviyo credits these four flows with 18,459 orders. Converge sees 1,140 orders worth €97,226 from sessions that actually began with a click on one of these emails. Klaviyo attributes any order placed inside the window after an open, and this flow reaches people who have just bought and are already primed to reorder — so most of that gap is revenue that would have landed anyway. Use Klaviyo revenue to rank emails against each other; use the Converge figure when someone asks what the flow is really worth.

Definitions and caveats

Stages are attributed independently, not nested

Each on-site stage is its own Klaviyo conversion metric, attributed to the send separately. A profile can show up in Started Checkout without appearing in Added to Cart — buy-now buttons, Shop Pay and direct checkout links skip the cart event. That is why 22 of 112 sends show more checkouts than carts and why step conversion can exceed 100%. Read each stage as its own count, not a strict subset of the one above.

  • Revenue is EUR, post-discount, pre-cost — the Shopify Placed Order conversion value attributed to each send. It is not margin. The calculator above is where cost gets applied.
  • Revenue per 1,000 delivered is the fair way to compare emails, because audience size falls sharply down the sequence.
  • AOV is attributed revenue divided by attributed orders, so it moves with basket mix as well as discount depth.
  • Delivered, not sent, is the denominator for every rate, matching Klaviyo. Recipients are in the KPI strip.
  • Opened and clicked are unique profiles. Open→click is Klaviyo's click-to-open rate.
  • Active is unique profiles firing Active on Site after the send — Klaviyo's nearest thing to a session count. Converge confirms it tracks unique clicks almost exactly at flow level.
  • Cart and checkout are the Shopify metrics as unique profiles. "Immediately after" is Klaviyo's attribution window, not a strict same-session rule.
  • Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens unevenly by market, so HU and NL open rates are not comparable to CZ's in absolute terms. Click, order and revenue figures are the safe comparison.
