Converge, at flow level

Converge maps Klaviyo down to the flow, not the individual email, so this cannot be split by email. It covers the whole flow (#1–#27), not just #16+. Its value is calibration: real sessions against Klaviyo unique clicks, and click-driven orders against Klaviyo's attributed orders.

The attribution gap

Klaviyo credits this flow with 18,459 orders across the four markets. Converge sees 1,140 orders from sessions that actually started with a click on one of these emails. Klaviyo attributes any order placed inside the window after an open, and this flow goes to people who have just bought and are already primed to reorder — so most of that 16× gap is orders that would likely have happened anyway. Use Klaviyo numbers to compare emails against each other, and the Converge number when you need the flow's real incremental contribution.

Definitions and caveats

Stages are attributed independently, not nested

Each on-site stage is a separate Klaviyo conversion metric attributed to the send on its own. A profile can appear in Started Checkout without appearing in Added to Cart — buy-now buttons, Shop Pay and direct checkout links skip the cart event. That is why 22 of 112 sends show more checkouts than carts, and why step-conversion percentages can exceed 100%. Read each stage as its own count, not as a strict subset of the one above.

  • Delivered, not sent, is the denominator for open rate, click rate and the on-site stages, matching Klaviyo. Recipients are in the KPI strip.
  • Opened and clicked are unique profiles — one profile counted once per email.
  • Open→click is Klaviyo's click-to-open rate: unique clicks ÷ unique opens.
  • Active = unique profiles who fired Active on Site after the send. This is the closest thing Klaviyo has to a session count, and Converge confirms it tracks unique clicks almost exactly at flow level.
  • Added to cart and started checkout use the Shopify metrics, unique profiles, attributed to the send — the "immediately after" you asked for is Klaviyo's attribution window, not a strict same-session rule.
  • Placed order is the conversion count on Shopify Placed Order. Where one profile ordered twice, orders exceed unique buyers.
  • Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates opens unevenly by market. HU and NL open rates are not comparable to CZ's in absolute terms; click and order rates are the safe comparison.
  • Revenue is post-discount, pre-COGS. The 25% arm's order advantage still has to clear the extra 10 points of discount in the contribution margin model.
